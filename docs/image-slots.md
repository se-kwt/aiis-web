# Image slots

Every image, video and decorative-art slot on the Shady Side homepage (SSA), as measured in `docs/reference/ssa-{1440,768,375}.json` (every key with a `src`). This is the replacement checklist: while the AIIS build hotlinks the SSA files by the absolute URL below (never downloaded), each photo carries the "Temp photo" tag, and each row's last column says what AIIS shot or art replaces it.

How to read it:
- Role: `logo` rows are brand marks replaced by the AIIS logo/crest PNGs. `photo` and `video` are the slots needing AIIS media. `art` / `art (CSS background)` are decorative SVGs (rebuilt or re-coloured by AIIS, not photographed). `container` rows are section wrappers whose `src` was only inherited from the first photo inside them (no slot of their own).
- Sizes are rendered CSS pixels (w x h) from the 1440 and 375 reference; 768 equals 375's layout for most slots. "hidden" = not rendered at that width.
- Fit/position is the computed `object-fit, object-position` of the image or video (for picture/figure/div hosts the inner `img` is reported, with its parallax transform at rest), or the `background` size, position, repeat for CSS art.
- Art colours: strokes/fills inside the SVGs (yellow #FFFF01 / #FFB81C / #FFC226 to Orange, green #1FFF01 and turquoise #11FEEE to Sky, navy #041E42 to Navy; white stays) are not in computed styles; see `tools/color-map.json`.
