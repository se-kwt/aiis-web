About Us

Our Story

We Nurture Tomorrow’s Global Citizens

Aspire Indian International School is one of Kuwait’s leading CBSE schools for Pre-KG through Grade 10, offering an academically rigorous, values-led education shaped around every child’s confidence, curiosity and character.

Founded to give Kuwait’s Indian community a school as ambitious as the futures its students would go on to build, AIIS opened with a handful of classrooms and one guiding promise: that every child would be known, encouraged and challenged by name. That promise still shapes the school we are today.

Our Mission

Aspire Indian International School inspires every learner to question boldly, care deeply, and lead with integrity.

What Guides Us

Respect, honesty and discipline sit alongside kindness and curiosity as the values every classroom at AIIS is built on, lived out in how students treat one another as much as in what they learn.

Looking Forward

As Kuwait’s community keeps growing, so do we — investing in our campus, our teachers and our programmes so every student leaves AIIS ready for a world that rewards adaptability as much as knowledge.

A Community That Knows Every Child

Small class sizes and attentive teachers mean no student is just a face in the crowd. At AIIS, learning is relational: teachers know each child’s strengths, stretch their thinking and notice when they need support.

Read Our Vision & Mission

A Richly Diverse Classroom

Our students come from across Kuwait’s Indian community and beyond, bringing languages, festivals and perspectives that make every classroom a place to understand the world, not just read about it.

Explore School Life

Ready for a Changing World

Alongside a strong CBSE foundation, students build the digital literacy, problem-solving and communication skills that prepare them for whatever comes after AIIS — in Kuwait or anywhere their ambitions take them.
