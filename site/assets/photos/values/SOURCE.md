# Values photo sources

Photos for the three pinned `value-fig` cards in the "values" section (CARING TEACHERS / GLOBAL OUTLOOK / VALUES-LED LEARNING), pulled from the live school site (aiiskwt.com) to replace the hotlinked `shadyside`/finalsite.net stock photos that were in `data-temp-photo` figures. These are small source files (305-518 px, same resolution as published on the live site) and should be swapped for proper photography when it's available.

- `caring-teachers-photo.jpg` (CARING TEACHERS): from `https://www.aiiskwt.com/images/young-leaders-cbse-aiis.jpg` (student leadership program page), 407x305. A teacher standing with two students in lab coats in a school science lab.
- `global-outlook-photo.jpg` (GLOBAL OUTLOOK): from `https://www.aiiskwt.com/galleryimage/117702004.jpg` (school photo gallery, World Heritage Day), 518x518. Students presenting at a World Heritage Day assembly, with a globe backdrop.
- `values-learning-photo.jpg` (VALUES-LED LEARNING): from `https://www.aiiskwt.com/galleryimage/1693963585.jpg` (school photo gallery), 1067x1067. A teacher with an arm around a row of sash-wearing students at a moral-values assembly (Gandhi's "see/speak/hear no evil" theme, with Hindi text on truth and honesty).
