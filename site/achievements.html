Achievements

Beyond the classroom, AIIS runs a steady calendar of small initiatives built around kindness, wellbeing and everyday habits — alongside the competitions its students regularly take part in.

Everyday initiatives

  • Meals for Smiles
  • Hydration Bell
  • Cup of Kindness
  • Morning Affirmations
  • Joy of Giving Week
  • Do It Yourself Week
  • In the Kitchen Week
  • Eye & Oral Care Workshops

On “Wednesdays of Giving,” for instance, students bring food from home to share with the school’s support staff — a small, recurring act that says thank you to the people usually working behind the scenes. A daily “Hydration Bell” reminds the whole campus to pause and drink water, and morning affirmations give students a steady, positive way to start the day.

Competing beyond AIIS

AIIS students regularly take part in inter-school events — from science and robotics exhibitions to art, recitation and cultural competitions — and have brought home placements and recognition across several of them.

About Us

AIIS is where your child can discover what they love and grow into who they want to be. We focus on academic progress alongside emotional, social and creative growth, building a school where every student is encouraged to reach their full potential.
